Five dimensions. One intelligent core.

The Star has always measured what a company is made of. Now it measures something more — whether intelligence runs at the center, or sits bolted on the edge. Same five points. A new core.

What changed

The old star radiated from essence.
The new one radiates from intelligence — because in this era, the essence of how a company operates is its AI.

AI is not a sixth point on the star. If it were just another spoke, it would be one more thing you bought — and the advantage would expire the day a competitor bought it too. It sits at the center, where Bistvo always lived, because a company that is truly AI-native doesn't have intelligence as a feature. It radiates everything else — mindset, leadership, strategy, brand, team — out of it.

The method

Spin the star. Each turn goes deeper.

The Star was never a snapshot — it's a lens you turn. The original five turns still hold. Only the destination changed: you no longer spin back to a stable essence. You spin toward an intelligent one.

TURN 1
See
The state as it is. Where does AI actually run today, and where is it still theatre bolted to the edge?
TURN 2
Look behind
What sits beneath each point — the real reason a dimension scores where it does, not the surface story.
TURN 3
See the drivers
Which point, if it reached the core first, would pull the others with it. The first domino.
TURN 4
Go to essence
Down into the center — what intelligence at the core would actually mean for how this company operates.
TURN 5
Radiate
From the intelligent core outward — redraw all five points as they become when they run from it.
